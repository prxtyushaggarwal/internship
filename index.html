LPU GROUND TRUTH 10+ Batch Interviews

Rule #1: Legitimate companies pay you for your labor. If an organization asks for even ₹100 as a "seat booking fee", "training charge", or "verification kit", it is 100% a fraudulent scheme.

Research Question Insight: Confidence erosion hurts student careers longer than momentary monetary loss.
STEP 02 OF VETTING

Verification Walkthrough: Know What to Check Next

Don't rely on gut feeling. Use this 4-step protocol before sending resumes, signing agreements, or spending hours on assignments.

Average time: 4 minutes
01

MCA Legal Search

Is the firm officially registered under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs in India (Private Limited / LLP)?

How to check: 1. Visit mca.gov.in
2. Go to "Master Data" → "View Company Master Data"
3. Check paid-up capital and director names.
Open MCA Portal
02

Employee & Alumni Audit

Do real humans work there? Search LinkedIn for people listed under the company page.

Look for: • Full-time CTO / Tech Leads
• Actual alumni from your college
• Red Flag: 5,000 "followers" but only 2 employees with blank profiles.
Search on LinkedIn
03

Domain Age & Email Header

Scammers often register a new domain 15 days before launching mass WhatsApp hiring broadcasts.

How to check: • WHOIS lookup for domain creation date
• If domain is less than 3 months old, treat with high suspicion.
• Verify contact email has same domain.
Lookup Domain on WHOIS
04

Unpaid Labor Boundary

Detect "ghost internships" where companies exploit students to build production code or sales lists for free.

The Rule: • Valid task: < 3-4 hours, dummy dataset, synthetic problem.
• Exploitation: "Build our client's complete CRM dashboard before interview".
Use Script to Clarify Task

ASSERTIVE STUDENT TOOLKIT

Recruiter Scripts & Email Templates

Don't know what to reply when a recruiter asks for money or gives a suspiciously vague offer? Copy and send these professional boundary-setting templates.

TEMPLATE 01 Refuse Upfront Fee

"Politely Declining Pay-to-Work Demands"

When asked to pay for a "certificate registration", "laptop security deposit", or "training slot".

Dear Recruiter, Thank you for considering my profile for the role. However, per University Placement Cell (T&P) regulations and ethical employment guidelines, students are strictly advised against paying any upfront slot reservation, training, or onboarding fees for internship programs. If this position requires any financial payment from candidates, please withdraw my application. Otherwise, I would be happy to proceed with standard technical evaluations.
TEMPLATE 02 Verify Legitimacy

"Asking for Corporate Email & Entity Details"

When contacted via WhatsApp, Telegram, or personal @gmail/@outlook accounts.

Dear Recruiter, Thank you for reaching out regarding this opportunity. To verify this role with our University Training & Placement department for internship credit/NOC records, could you please email me from your official corporate domain address (@companyname.com) along with the company's registered Corporate Identification Number (CIN / LLPIN)? Looking forward to hearing from you through the official channel.
TEMPLATE 03 Ghost Task Boundary

"Clarifying Scope of Excessive Take-Home Tasks"

When asked to build a full commercial feature or scrape live data prior to any interview.

Dear Team, I reviewed the take-home assessment. Given the scope involves production-grade modules that require over 20 hours of engineering, I would like to clarify the evaluation structure. I have already published open-source repositories and portfolio projects demonstrating these exact competencies (attached below). Could we schedule a 20-minute technical screening call to review my existing code before I undertake an extended assignment?

Safe Limits & University Guidelines (INT335 P24 Boundary)

VerifyIntern provides decision support to guide student judgement; it does not guarantee legal legitimacy. If you suspect criminal fraud, notify university Training & Placement authorities and report immediately to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).

INT335 · LPU · PROJECT P24

Design Thinking Solution Architecture

How this web tool implements the 5 design thinking phases.

01 EMPATHIZE Conducted 10+ student interviews across LPU searchers, seniors, scam victims, and T&P staff. Identified that confidence loss causes worse long-term career paralysis than direct financial loss.
02 DEFINE (POV & HMW) "Students searching for internships need a quick, trustworthy way to assess an opportunity because peer experiences are scattered."
How might we help students judge quickly whether an opportunity is real and worth it?
03 IDEATE & SCREEN Integrated 4 core concepts: Red-Flag Automated Checklist, Senior Experience Bank, MCA/Domain Verification Walkthrough, and Recruiter Response Scripts.
04 PROTOTYPE (THIS INTERACTIVE PORTAL) Engineered a 4-step live flow: Input Listing → Auto-Detect Missing Details → Step-by-Step Vetting Action → Clear Judgement.
05 TEST & SAFE LIMITS Evaluation criteria: Task clarity, Next action clarity, Reasoning quality, and Safe limits awareness ("Decision support, not a guarantee").
